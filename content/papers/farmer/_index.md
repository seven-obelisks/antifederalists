# The Federal Farmer

Federal Farmer was the pseudonym used by an anonymous Anti-Federalist writer during the U.S. Constitution ratification debates. The authorship is often attributed to Richard Henry Lee, a Virginia statesman.

Federal Farmer’s essays opposed the Constitution, arguing it would centralize power and weaken state authority. He criticized the lack of a Bill of Rights, fearing that without such protections, the new government could become tyrannical. Federal Farmer called for amendments to safeguard individual liberties and maintain the balance of power between the states and the federal government.
