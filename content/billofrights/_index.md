# Bill of Rights

### The Anti-Federalists played a vital role in shaping the Bill of Rights, the first ten amendments protecting individual freedoms. During the Constitution’s ratification debates, they feared the new strong central government could easily abuse power without explicit protections.

### Though initially resisted by Federalists who trusted the Constitution’s structure, Anti-Federalist pressure led to the Bill of Rights’ adoption. The Bill of Rights ultimately limited government power and ensured citizens’ legal protections, reflecting the Anti-Federalists’ key influence in securing individual freedoms within the new republic.
