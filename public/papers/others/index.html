The Scourge

The Scourge is a satirical Anti-Federalist essay published in the Carlisle Gazette condemning Federalist celebrations following Pennsylvania’s ratification of the Constitution. The author warns that unchecked national power will suppress liberty and mocks Federalist elites for abandoning republican principles.

One of the People

One of the People is a scathing Anti-Federalist essay published in the Carlisle Gazette attacking Federalist manipulation of public events to advance their agenda. The author exposes their hypocrisy, warns of their authoritarian ambitions, and mocks their deceitful tactics in undermining republican values.
