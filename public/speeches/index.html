Patrick Henry

Patrick Henry was a key Anti-Federalist leader, known for his fiery speeches against the U.S. Constitution. He argued that the Constitution would centralize power, threatening states’ rights and individual freedoms. Henry also criticized the lack of a Bill of Rights, fearing it would lead to tyranny.
